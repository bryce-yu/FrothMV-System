# Dataset Summary

## Raw dataset location

The full Kaggle froth-bubble segmentation dataset is stored locally
outside the FrothMV-System repository and outside OneDrive.

The location is configured through the `FROTH_DATASET_ROOT`
environment variable.

## Folder structure

The dataset contains three predefined splits:

- `train/train_images`
- `train/train_masks`
- `valid/valid_images`
- `valid/valid_masks`
- `test/test_images`
- `test/test_masks`

All images and masks are stored as PNG files.

## Visual mask review

A sample of image-mask pairs was inspected from the train (5), valid (3) and test (3) splits using 'src/visualise_dataset_samples.py'.

The review displayed:

- the original froth image
- the supplied ground-truth mask
- contours extracted from the mask overlaid on the original image

### General observations

- As was noted in the Project 1 Milestone Report, although the outlines do not perfectly follow the rounded shape of every bubble they do show the majority of the area covered by each bubble.
- Image and mask dimensions were consistent for the inspected samples.
- Contours provide usable approximate bubble boundaries within the froth surface.
- Some neighbouring bubbles may be represented as connected regions.
- Some boundaries are irregular due to lighting, glare and unclear bubble edges
- Overall, the masks appear suitable for general bubble count and size estimation.

### Limitations observed

- In numerous cases, shadows can cover up to the whole bottom half of the tank/cell surface in the image - whilst this may not impact average bubble size estimation too much it will impact bubble count if comparing an image where the full surface is visible versus only half visible.
- As well as shadows, resolution or level of blur seems to vary depending on image, which has some impact on the accuracy of the masks. 
- In some (maybe half) of the sample images, masks suggest some bubbles exist outside the froth surface, which shouldn't be possible. Looking at the original image does not seem to be glare specifically, so likely a shortcoming/error of the segmentation mask algorithm used. But since the Kaggle authors do specify the use of YOLOv8 Segment Anything models likely will just accept this. 

### Decision

The dataset will be retained for development of the YOLOv8 segmentation and bubble-metric extraction pipeline.
